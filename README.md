# 📊 TradeFlow — AI for Naija Market Women
### Built in Otukpo, Benue State

> **LIVE:** https://trade-flow-7xa5-chi.vercel.app/ 
> **FREE FOREVER • No Paystack needed**

TradeFlow helps market women for Otukpo Main Market track sales, profit, best seller and ask AI wetin to stock.

## ✨ Features (v4.0)

**🔐 FREE Login**
- Each shop get her own data (Terkula's Shop, Mama Nkechi etc)
- Shop Name + Phone + Password
- No payment wall

**📊 Dashboard**
- Total Revenue: ₦11.3M+ (real data)
- Profit: Na Your Gain (70% margin calc locally)
- Best Seller: Rice HOT - 45% revenue
- Otukpo Insight Yellow Card
- Share to 【entity-WhatsApp¦canonical_name=WhatsApp】

**📦 Sales**
- Add Sale: Product, Qty, Price
- **NEW v4: Product Photo** - snap Rice bag with camera
- Recent Sales list with photo thumbnails
- Delete, totals, avg sale

**🧾 Receipts (NEW v4)**
- Each sale get Receipt button
- Print receipt: Product, Qty x Price, Total, Date, Shop
- Share receipt to customer WhatsApp
- Daily Sales Report

**🤖 AI**
- Ask: "Wetin I suppose stock? Why profit high?"
- Voice Input 🎤 - Speak Pidgin
- Answers in simple English + small Pidgin
- Share AI advice to WhatsApp

**📱 Interactive App**
- Bottom Nav: Dashboard | Sales | AI | Profile
- Works offline (localStorage)
- Mobile-first, Otukpo market ready

## 🛠️ Stack
- Frontend: Single HTML file (no build), vanilla JS, CSS
- Backend: Python FastAPI on Render - https://trade-flow-brw1.onrender.com
- AI: Groq FREE (qwen/qwen3-32b) with fallback
- Data: 80+ real market sales (Rice, Garri, Maize, Cement, Oil etc)
